01

Overview

Arrow Puzzle does not require an account, does not ask you to enter personal information, and does not run its own servers or backend — everything the game itself needs to function is generated on your device or bundled with the app.

The one exception is advertising: the app shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob (as an independent data controller) collects a limited set of technical identifiers to select and measure those ads. Section 03 below covers exactly what that involves.

02

Data stored on your device

The app saves your gameplay state using Android's standard local-storage mechanism (shared_preferences), entirely on your own device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate:

  • Level progress — which level you're currently on, and which levels you've completed.
  • Lives — your remaining lives and when they last refilled.
  • Hints — how many hints you have left.
  • Coins & rewards — in-game currency earned from levels and the daily spin.
  • Daily spin status — whether today's free spin has already been claimed.

Uninstalling the app removes this data along with it, since it never leaves your device.

03

Advertising & AdMob

Arrow Puzzle uses Google AdMob to display banner ads (shown at the bottom of some screens), interstitial ads (shown between levels), and rewarded ads (optional, watched in exchange for in-game rewards). To select relevant ads, measure how they perform, and guard against invalid activity, AdMob and its partner networks may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android's AD_ID) and general device information (model, OS version).
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Ad interaction data — impressions, clicks, and how long an ad was viewed.

This collection is performed by Google and its advertising partners, not by Arrow Puzzle directly, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data collection disclosure. Where required by your region, you can manage ad personalization through your device's ad settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices) or through Google's Ads Settings.

Note: ads may be temporarily unavailable while the app's advertising configuration is being finalized. This does not change what's described above once ads are active.
04

App permissions

Arrow Puzzle requests the following Android permissions, each used only for the purpose listed:

  • android.permission.INTERNET Required Lets the app request and display ads from AdMob's servers.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required Lets AdMob adapt ad requests to your current connectivity.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Required Lets AdMob read the device's advertising identifier, used for ad targeting and frequency capping.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or photo-library access.

05

Children's privacy

Arrow Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If AdMob's mixed-audience settings apply to your listing, ad requests are configured accordingly at the Play Console level to serve only non-personalized ads where required by applicable law (including COPPA).

06

Data retention & deletion

Because gameplay data (Section 02) lives only on your device, you control its lifetime directly:

  • To reset progress — use the in-app restart option, where offered, or clear the app's storage from Android's Settings → Apps → Arrow Puzzle → Storage.
  • To delete everything — uninstall the app; no copy remains anywhere else.

Data collected by AdMob is retained and managed under Google's own retention policies, independent of this app — see the AdMob disclosure linked in Section 03 for how to request access to or deletion of that data.
